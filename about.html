Story of the Bakery

Founded in 2018, North Star Bakery started as a small neighborhood passion project with a simple goal: to bring people together through the comforting smell and taste of authentic, handmade bread.

Sourcing Philosophy

We partner exclusively with local family farms and organic millers in our region. Quality ingredients mean better flavor and a stronger, more sustainable local community.

Staff Highlights

Head Baker Elena brings over 15 years of European artisan baking experience, while our front-of-house team ensures every customer is greeted with a warm smile and personalized recommendation.
